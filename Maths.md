MSE - Mean Squared Error
Example:
Suppose the actual values are:

Actual Y
---------
10
20
30

And your model predicts:

Predicted Y
-----------
12
17
33

The errors are:

Prediction - Actual

12 - 10 =  2
17 - 20 = -3
33 - 30 =  3

MSE squares these errors:

2²  = 4
(-3)² = 9
3²  = 9

Then takes the average:

MSE = (4+9+9}/3
MSE = 7.33

So:

MSE gives the model a numerical measurement of how wrong its predictions are.

The goal during training is:

Minimize MSE
